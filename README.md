# 🔒 Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 25, 2026
**Developer / Publisher:** Digital Craft Labs
**Contact:** digitalcraftapps@gmail.com

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Digital Craft Labs ("we," "our," or "us") handles information when you use the mobile application Digital Craft 2048 (the "Application"). We keep data collection to what the game needs, and we do not sell your personal information.

The game can be played offline. Some features need an internet connection and use the services described below: signing in with Google, leaderboards, online duels, power packs (in-app purchases) and rewarded ads.

## 2. Information We Collect and Store

### 2.1 Stored only on your device

Your game progress, current board, scores, best score, settings, the powers you hold and a default player name (for example "Player4821") are stored on your device using standard local storage. This data stays on your device unless you use the online features described below.

### 2.2 Signing in with Google (optional)

If you choose to sign in, Google shares your Google account name, email address, profile picture and a unique account ID with us through Firebase Authentication. We never receive your Google password. We use this information to recognise your account, to show your first name and the initial of your last name on leaderboards and in duels, and to save your powers to your account. Your email address and profile picture are not shown to other players.

### 2.3 Playing as a guest

You do not need an account to play duels or to post a score. Guests use a temporary anonymous ID created by Firebase and the default player name. We do not receive your name or email address.

### 2.4 Online game data

When you use leaderboards or duels, we store the following in Google Cloud Firestore:

- Your display name (your Google first name and last initial, or your default guest name), your best score and your weekly best score. Other players can see these on the leaderboards.
- Your power balances (swaps, breaks, doubles, bombs and jokers), for signed-in accounts.
- Your duel win, loss and draw record, for signed-in Google accounts only.
- Your online status: your display name, whether you are a guest, and when you were last active, so other players can find you for a duel.
- Temporary duel information: player IDs, display names and live scores. We delete this after the match and give it an expiry time so that anything left over is removed automatically.

### 2.5 Purchases

Power packs are bought through Google Play. Google processes the payment, and we never see or store your card or other payment details. We receive confirmation of what you purchased so that we can add the powers.

### 2.6 Advertising

The Application shows rewarded ads, which you choose to watch to earn a free power. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and operating-system details, and how you interact with the ad. They use it to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where you have allowed it, personalise ads. We do not receive this information.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), you will be asked for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (usually under Settings → Google → Ads). You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

### 2.7 What we do not collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos or files, camera or microphone data, or phone number. The Application asks for internet access so that the online features can work. Google's advertising and billing services may also use related technical permissions, such as network state and the advertising ID.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above only to:

- provide the game and its online features: sign-in, leaderboards, duels and saving your powers;
- process purchases and restore the powers you have bought;
- show rewarded ads and let Google measure them;
- keep the game fair and secure, and prevent misuse; and
- reply to your questions and requests.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use analytics or attribution services such as Firebase Analytics, Adjust or AppsFlyer.

## 5. What Other Players Can See

Other players can see your display name, your scores, your online status and your duel results. They cannot see your email address or profile picture. If you do not want your Google first name to appear, sign out and play as a guest.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher age that applies where you live). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

## 7. Data Retention and Account Deletion

- **Data on your device** is deleted when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
- **Account and online data** (your Firebase account record, display name, scores, powers and duel record) is kept while your account is active and is deleted when you ask us to delete it.
- **Duel data and online status** are temporary. We delete them after a match, and they also expire automatically.

### How to delete your account and data

Email us at digitalcraftapps@gmail.com from the email address of your Google account, with the subject "Delete my 2048 account". We will delete your account and the online data linked to it within 30 days and confirm by email. This also removes the powers saved to your account, and they cannot be restored. Your purchase history stays with Google Play, and refunds follow Google Play's policies.

If you played as a guest and want your leaderboard entry removed, email us your default player name (for example "Player4821") and we will remove it.

## 8. Security

Data sent between the Application and our services is encrypted in transit. Access to the online data is restricted by security rules so that each player can change only their own information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Your Choices and Rights

- You can play without an account, or sign out at any time and continue as a guest.
- You can remove the game's access to your Google account at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
- You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings.
- You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing us.

Depending on where you live (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California), you may have additional rights, such as withdrawing consent or complaining to your local data protection authority. Contact us to use any of these rights.

## 10. International Data Transfers

Google runs the services listed above on servers in several countries, so your information may be processed outside the country where you live.
