1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy outlines how Digital Craft Labs ("we," "our," or "us") handles information when you use the mobile application Digital Craft 2048 (the "Application").

We respect your privacy and are committed to maintaining a transparent, secure, and privacy-first experience for all users.

2. Information Collection and Storage

  • Personal Data: The Application does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, physical location, device identifiers, or contact lists).
  • Device Permissions: The Application runs offline and does not request any sensitive device permissions, including camera, microphone, precise location, contacts, or shared storage.
  • Local Game Data: Gameplay state, current moves, active scores, and highest scores are stored solely on your device using standard local storage mechanisms. This information never leaves your device and is not transmitted across the internet.

3. Third-Party Services and Tracking

Digital Craft 2048 does not integrate any third-party behavioral tracking, advertising networks, or analytics SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics, Adjust, or AppsFlyer).

If Google Play Services are accessed by your Android operating system to manage updates or installation, such interactions are governed directly by the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Children’s Privacy

The Application does not collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age threshold in your jurisdiction), complying with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy.

5. Data Retention and Security

Because all game settings and high scores are stored strictly within the local application sandbox on your device, clearing the app's cache/storage or uninstalling the app permanently deletes all stored progress.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected on this page alongside a revised "Effective Date."
